Bundled webfonts
===============

inter-latin-var.woff2
  Inter (variable, weights 400-700), latin subset.
  Copyright (c) Rasmus Andersson. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/rsms/inter

geist-mono-latin-var.woff2
  Geist Mono (variable, weights 400-600), latin subset.
  Copyright (c) Vercel. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/vercel/geist-font

Both files are served from the app bundle so Rheow renders correctly offline
and satisfies the Content Security Policy in src-tauri/tauri.conf.json.
